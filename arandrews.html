Not much is known about the A.R Andrews bottling works But we do know it began in 1905 in Delray with a man named Albert Robert Andrew who ran his bottling works with his sons often known as A.R .ANDREWS or A.R Andrews and sons bottling works, and A.R Andrews Registerd They had their own signature flavors Ginger Ale, Orange Cider, Root beer, Lemon Sour and Strawberry pop most notibly and their most popular beverage Kolo Cream witch was produced for almost 100 years until kars final closure in the year 2000 Kolo Cream would have been 95 years old though originally made with saccharin replaced with cane sugar in the 1940s but the andrews had many different bottle molds and types of bottles made by glassblowers i believe they made their own bottles all the way until they sold out to john kar i could be wrong though The Detroit Urban Archeology Society has never seen a machine made Andrews bottle and thats about all we know about The A.R Andrews other then it was on lyle street.
