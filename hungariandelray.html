For a period of nearly 70 years Delray wasnt just any american neighborhood it was one of the largest Hungarian Communitys in the entire united states and hungarian was the primary language for most people lving in delray during this time. Delray wasnt just a place some hungarians once lived it was a place they could bring their culture to and build on they brought their langauge their relgion you can still see it in the Hungarian Churches in Delray today they created a large semi industrial community with lots of job opportunities and a mixture of hungarian american culture that was completely unique to delray.
